### Contrast check

All preview candidates were generated to clear WCAG AA for body text and key UI surfaces. The selected palette keeps strong separation between deep background planes and high-contrast near-white text, with violet reserved for actions and proof-state emphasis.

## Typography - Inter + UI Monospace

- **Display + body:** Inter
- **Mono (numbers, addresses, code):** system UI monospace stack

Current implementation uses Google-hosted Inter in the HTML files and `ui-monospace` for commitments, proof statuses, and invoice identifiers.

## Gradients

Two restrained gradients are used in the current HTML screens:

- Background atmosphere: deep black-violet fields with subtle radial violet glow
- Action emphasis: violet-to-orchid gradients for brand marks and primary CTAs

Use them sparingly. This product should feel credible first, expressive second.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Use direct, specific, number-forward language. Prefer “verify”, “register”, “settle”, “prove”, and “disclose” over vague benefit copy. The product should sound like privacy infrastructure for real financial workflows, not a retail crypto app.

### Words to avoid

Avoid hype terms such as “unleash”, “supercharge”, “revolutionary”, and “game-changing”. Avoid playful fintech slang, excessive excitement, and any copy that makes privacy sound magical instead of mechanically trustworthy.

### Voice example

> Settle the invoice on Stellar. Publish the proof, not the amount.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use dark, low-noise surfaces and keep the bright violet mostly for actions, selected states, and emphasis.
- Keep invoice ranges, proof status, and commitment displays visually distinct from ordinary marketing copy.
- Use monospace for IDs, hashes, and metric values that users will scan repeatedly.
- Let the product story stay narrow and legible: contract bounds public, actual amount private.

**Don't:**

- Revert to generic wallet or portfolio language that hides the invoice-settlement use case.
- Overuse purple across every background block.
- Introduce warm playful gradients or meme-like crypto styling.
- Turn the dashboard into a token-trading screen. The core story is settlement plus proof verification.
